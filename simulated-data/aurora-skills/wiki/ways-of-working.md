# Ways of Working — Aurora Skills

This page is the team's working agreement. Much of it was written *after* the Q2 incident retro
(2026-05-05) and the "how we work together" retro (2026-06-02), and put into practice in Q3.

## Psychological safety / the andon cord

- **Pulling the cord early is the expected behavior, and we celebrate it.** Surfacing a mistake or
  a risk fast is a win, not a failure. (The Q2 validator incident took 4 days to surface because it
  felt unsafe to say; the comparable Q3 regression took 41 minutes.)
- **Blameless incident notes** on every incident (AUR-12). We fix the system, not the person.
- **The failures are the interesting things.** A green build teaches nobody anything.

## Healthy conflict — the measurable behaviors

We treat "healthy conflict" as countable, not a vibe. The signals we watch (and now measure with
the insights tool, PRD 05):

1. **Everyone speaks on a decision; no one holds more than ~40% of talk-time.**
2. **More questions asked than assertions made.** *Questions are participation* — a person who asks
   more than they assert is not low-value.
3. **At least one person states out loud that they changed their mind.**
4. **Dissent arrives before the decision, not in the hallway after.** Late objections trend to zero.
5. **The same disagreement is not reopened a third time** — meaning it was settled, not suppressed.

## Meeting format (Q3 onward)

- **Round-robin + async pre-reads** (AUR-13). Everyone present takes a turn. This was introduced
  specifically because one teammate (Naomi) thinks best in writing and the old broadcast format
  rendered her contribution invisible. Silence was a *format* problem, not a person problem.
- The facilitator gets a **private nudge** when one speaker passes ~40% airtime, or when someone
  present hasn't spoken. Nudges are private; participation is never scored publicly.

## The insights tool (PRD 05) — guardrails

- It reports **patterns, not verdicts about people**. It advises; humans decide.
- It **cross-references chat** before calling anyone disengaged.
- It has a **quiet mode** (added after it over-flagged a low-activity planning week).

## Team-health model

We reference Lencioni's five dysfunctions (trust → conflict → commitment → accountability →
results) as an *ordered* stack: fix trust first, or every layer above it regresses. This is why the
Q2 work started with psychological safety before trying to measure anything.
