## Priority Actions (highest dysfunction first)

1. **[Trust]** Make surfacing mistakes safe. Adopt blameless incident notes and explicitly
   celebrate pulling the andon cord early, so problems are raised in hours, not days.
2. **[Conflict]** Bring dissent into the room before decisions. Balance airtime with a facilitation
   nudge or round-robin so objections and quieter voices surface before commitment, not in side
   channels afterward.
3. **[Commitment]** Close decisions explicitly with a written reopening bar (new information
   required) so settled questions stop being relitigated.
4. **[Accountability]** Normalize horizontal peer feedback and eliminate single points of failure —
   e.g., onboard a second security reviewer so review no longer depends on one person.
5. **[Results]** Weigh decisions against the shared team outcome over individual optics, and
   attribute wins to the team rather than individuals.
