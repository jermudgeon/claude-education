The dataset's whole point is the before and after on one team. These are the repo's computed figures from _comparison/before_after.json, which is what a scorer has to reproduce before anyone should believe a trend line.

Why this matters more than the scores. A ground-truth table is an accuracy test, not just sample data. It turns "does the scorer work" from an opinion into a measurement, and it is the thing that decides whether the trend feature can ship.
